About the Artist

Hi! I'm Cate. I am currently an advertising and design student at the University of San Francisco. I am from the Bay Area, and I love living in SF. There are so many fun things to do! Ever since I was little, I have always enjoyed drawing and being creative, and I still do today. My hobby is painting and drawing. Although I do not get to do it super often anymore, I want to start making more time for it, since it is what I love to do. I often paint with my Grandmother, because she also shared a passion for art. She has been a huge inspiration to me and has taught me a lot about painting and creativity. Some of my favorite things to paint are food, flowers, and places. I feel that I am often inspired by the world around me.
