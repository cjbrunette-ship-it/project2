This painting was one that I painted with my grandma again. We got inspired by a famous artist named Wayne Thiebaud, who paints a lot of food in a fun and colorful way. We ended up going to a grocery store to pick out fun looking pastries and we each picked out a different mini tart. This is one of my favorites because it was fun to mimic the style of another famous artist, and also it was meaningful time with my grandma.
