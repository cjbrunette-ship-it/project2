This is a drawing I did in my high school art class. We had to make a series of different pieces based on a theme, and my theme was childhood/nostalgia. I chose to draw the Golden Gate bridge because I grew up in Marin and would drive over it frequently, so that was a big part of my childhood. This is one of my favorites because now that I go to school in San Francisco it feels like a big symbol/part of my life.
