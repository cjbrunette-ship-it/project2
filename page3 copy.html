This is a still life painting that I made from a real cut-up watermelon. I painted this with my grandma at her house who also loves painting. It was during the summertime which is why we chose to paint a watermelon. This is one of my favorite paintings because I think it turned out really well, and it represents summertime well. Now whenever I look at this painting, I remember that summer and the time with my grandma.

  • Medium: Gouache Paint
  • Year Created: 2024
